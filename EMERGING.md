## Selection criteria

Projects listed here have public source code, an identifiable implementation, and a practical OSINT or investigative use case. Early development, limited adoption, and low star counts do not automatically exclude a project.

Membership in this view is stored in `Source Files`. Every project retains one of the 12 main `Categories` values and its concrete `Target Input` values in the canonical CSV.
